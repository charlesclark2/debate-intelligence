Debate is a club, open to any student who wants to join. It is a set of competitive events in
which students research a question, build the case for each side of it, and argue it in front of
a judge. The team competes in three of them: Policy debate, Lincoln-Douglas debate and Public
Forum debate. Each one is explained in plain language on
[what the events are](/events/).

If you would rather start with the practical questions, travel, costs, time and what parents are
asked to do, go straight to the [parent questions](/faq/).

Questions are welcome at any time, from students and from parents.
[Contact the coaches](/contact/).
