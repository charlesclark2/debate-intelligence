Earlier is better, because the season starts in early October, but nobody is turned away for
showing up late.

## Practice

| When | Where |
|---|---|
| Mondays and Wednesdays, during the lunch period | Whitefish Bay High School |
| Thursdays, after school | Whitefish Bay High School |

Practice is where cases get written, evidence gets found and rounds get run against teammates.

## What to bring on the first day

- Something to take notes on. A Chromebook is ideal.
- A good attitude.
- An open mind.
- A willingness to debate.

That is the whole list, and a student does not have to know which event they want yet.

## Three ways to sign up

1. **Email Coach Clark** at <charles.clark@wfbschools.org>.
2. **Come to a practice.** Any of the three above. No notice needed.
3. **Join the team's Remind group** with the code @debatewfb, which is how practice changes,
   tournament deadlines and travel details reach students and families.

Parents are welcome to email on a student's behalf, and welcome to come to the
[parent information session](/) on October 1.

## What happens next

New debaters learn one event first, practice rounds against teammates, and go to their first
tournament when they and the coach agree they are ready. Students choose which tournaments they
attend; a typical Wisconsin tournament is a Saturday at a host high school, arriving around
8:00 AM and competing into the afternoon.

The [parent questions](/faq/) page covers costs, travel, the time commitment and what parents are
asked to do.
