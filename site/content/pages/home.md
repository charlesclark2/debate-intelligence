purpose through rigorous research, strategic argumentation, and a strong team culture.

## New to this?

Debate is a club, open to any student who wants to join. It is a set of competitive events in
which students research a question, build the case for each side of it, and argue it in front of
a judge. The team competes in three of them: Policy
debate, Lincoln-Douglas debate and Public Forum debate. Each one is explained in plain language on
[what the events are](/events/).

If you would rather start with the practical questions, travel, costs, time and what parents are
asked to do, go straight to the [parent questions](/faq/).

## Joining

Debate is open to every student in grades 9 through 12, with no experience required and no tryout.
Students can join at any point in the fall semester, though earlier is better. See
[how to join](/join/) for practice times and the three ways to sign up.
