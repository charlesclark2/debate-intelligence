Whitefish Bay High School has a competitive debate team. Students research a year-long topic,
build arguments on both sides of it, and travel to tournaments to argue them in front of judges.

This site is being built. The pages that explain the events, how to join, what a season costs and
what it asks of a family are still being written, and the coaches will review them before they go
up.

## In the meantime

If you have a question about the team, please contact the coaches through the high school office.
