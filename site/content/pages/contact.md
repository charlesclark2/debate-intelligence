Email is the way to reach the team. There is no form on this page and nothing is stored: your
message goes to a coach's school email address and nowhere else.

## Coaches

**Charlie Clark, Head Coach**
<charles.clark@wfbschools.org>

Write to Coach Clark about joining the team, practice, tournaments, travel, costs, financial
support, parent judging, or anything on this site that is wrong or out of date.

## The school

If you would rather go through the school, contact the Whitefish Bay High School activities
office and they can pass a message on.

## Taking something off this site

Anyone may ask for anything on this site to be removed, without giving a reason. Email Coach Clark
and it comes down. A student's name or photograph is removed within 24 hours of the request.
