## Charlie Clark, Head Coach

Coach Clark has twenty years of coaching experience. He debated in college at the University of
Missouri-Kansas City and finished in the top 16 in the nation in his senior year.

He has qualified many students to national tournaments, including several top-10 finishers at the
National Speech and Debate Association national tournament, at the National Catholic Forensic
League national tournament and at the Tournament of Champions.

Coach Clark donates his entire debate coaching stipend back to the team.

Email: <charles.clark@wfbschools.org>

## Talking to a coach

Questions from parents and from students are welcome at any time, whether or not a student has
joined. Families who need financial support to take part should contact Coach Clark directly;
that conversation stays between the family and the coach.
