    Keys

    Enter or SpaceAccept the row as shown: check it and move on
    p h b t cPOCKET, HAT, BLOCK, TAG, CITE: set it, check the row, move on
    e a u oEVIDENCE, ANALYTIC, UNDERTAG, OTHER: set it, check the row, move on
    nStart a card on this row (its TAG, or its CITE when it has no tag), or stop one starting here. Then accept or set the unit
    kThe card's completeness: FULL, ABBREVIATED, CITE_ONLY, and for a block's first or last card, cut off by the block's edge (left unnumbered)
    Backspace or ←Back one row

    Holding a key down does nothing more than pressing it once. Progress is saved after every key: close the tab and stop the labeller with Ctrl+C whenever you like, and it starts where you stopped.
