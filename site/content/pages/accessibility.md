This site aims to meet Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 level AA.

## What that means here

Every page can be read and operated with a keyboard alone. Each page starts with a link that
skips past the navigation, the element that has keyboard focus is always outlined, and the
navigation menu on a small screen opens and closes with the keyboard as well as with a tap.

Text colors are checked against the background they sit on so that ordinary body text and links
meet the contrast level the guidelines ask for. Pages are laid out for a phone first and do not
need horizontal scrolling.

The site loads no advertising, no analytics tags and no social media widgets, and it downloads no
fonts or scripts from other companies. Nothing on the site tracks visitors.

## If something does not work

If a page is hard to read or to use, or a link or control does not work with your keyboard or
screen reader, please contact the coaches through the high school office and describe what went
wrong. We will fix it.
