## Before coaching

Coach Clark debated in college at the University of Missouri-Kansas City and finished in the top
16 in the nation in his senior year.

## Where he has coached

- **2007 to 2008, Kansas City Central High School, Missouri.**
- **2008 to 2009, Olathe North High School, Kansas.** His own high school.
- **2009 to 2011, Neenah High School, Wisconsin.**
- **2011 to 2012, Olathe North High School, Kansas.** National Speech and Debate Association
  (NSDA) national runner-up in Policy debate.
- **2012 to 2013, Blue Valley West High School, Kansas.** Set the Kansas record for Tournament of
  Champions bids at four; top 16 at National Catholic Forensic League (NCFL) nationals; state
  champions in Policy debate.
- **2013 to 2014, Marquette University High School, Wisconsin.** Director of Debate; the school's
  first Public Forum bid to the Tournament of Champions; state semifinalists.
- **2016 to 2019, Jefferson City High School, Missouri.** State champions; top 16 at the NCFL
  national tournament; top 16 at the NSDA national tournament.
- **2024 to now, Whitefish Bay High School, Wisconsin.** Assistant coach from 2024 to 2025, and
  head coach since.

## Away from the team

Outside of coaching, Coach Clark works full time at a sports analytics hedge fund and is
completing an MBA and an MS in Finance at Indiana University. He skis, plays tennis, and spends a
good deal of time with his dog, Skye.
